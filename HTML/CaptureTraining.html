Training capture — 5 samples per letter

For each letter A–Z, sign the letter and click Capture five times (vary your hand angle / position / lighting between shots). Each capture saves a JPEG frame, the 21-point MediaPipe landmarks, and detected handedness. When all 130 samples are taken, click Download JSON — output is a single bundle file you can feed to a training pipeline.
